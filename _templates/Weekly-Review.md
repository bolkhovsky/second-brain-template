## 📈 Состояние проектов
```dataview
TABLE 
  status as "Статус",
  priority as "Приоритет",
  file.mtime as "Обновлено"
FROM "Clients" OR "Efforts"
WHERE contains(tags, "#project/active")
SORT priority DESC
```

### Проекты, не обновлявшиеся >14 дней
```dataview
LIST
FROM "Efforts"
WHERE contains(tags, "#project/active")
AND file.mtime < date(today) - dur(14 days)
```
